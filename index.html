MEPCO Consumer Guides

Bill samajhne aur overbilling se bachne ki zaroori maloomat.

Bill Par Reference No Kahan Hai?

Bill ke sab se oopar baayein (top-left) janib ek box hota hai jisme 14 digits hote hain aur aakhir me U (Urban/Unprotected) ya R likha hota hai. Iske barabar 10-digit Consumer ID bhi likhi hoti hai.

Protected vs Unprotected Consumer

Agar pichle 6 maah tak aapka bill 200 units ya us se kam raha ho toh aap Protected rehte hain jahan per unit rate kam hota hai. Agar lagatar limit cross ho toh consumer Unprotected ho jata hai.

Meter Reading Aur Overbilling

Bill par darj current reading ko apne digital meter ki reading se milayein. Agar bill par reading zyada likhi ho, toh meter ki picture bana kar foran sub-division SDO office me darj karwayein.

Naya Connection (ENC Portal)

Naye connection ke liye online enc.com.pk portal par apply kiya jata hai:

  1. Application form online fill karein aur tracking number hasil karein.
  2. CNIC, hamsaye ke bill ki copy aur registry/malkiat dastawizat upload karein.
  3. MEPCO sarvey ke baad Demand Notice jari hoga jise bank me jama karwayein.
  4. Paid slip upload hone ke baad meter nasb kiya jata hai.
